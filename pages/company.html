Our Story

Rooted in Passion, Blooming with Purpose

Florista began in a small greenhouse with a big dream — to bring the beauty of nature into every home, every celebration, and every heart. Founded by Emily and Marcus Chen in 2014, what started as a weekend farmer's market stand has grown into a beloved floral destination trusted by thousands.

We partner directly with sustainable flower farms to ensure every bloom that arrives at your door is freshly cut, ethically sourced, and handled with the utmost care. Our team of expert florists curates each arrangement by hand, putting love into every stem.

✦ Our Mission & Values ✦

Everything we do is guided by our commitment to beauty, sustainability, and meaningful human connection.

Sustainability

We believe in protecting the planet that gives us our flowers. Every step from farm to delivery is designed to minimize our environmental footprint.

Passion

Flowers have the power to transform moments. We put our hearts into every arrangement because we believe in the emotional power of blooms.

Integrity

We promise what we deliver. From farm-fresh quality to on-time delivery — honesty and reliability are the cornerstones of our brand.

Excellence

We never settle. Each bouquet is crafted to the highest standard, ensuring every customer receives something truly extraordinary.
